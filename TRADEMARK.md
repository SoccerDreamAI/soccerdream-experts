## English

### Why this file exists

A source-available repository invites two different things, and they deserve different
answers:

- **Copying the files.** Governed by `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md`. Enforceable, because we hold
  the copyright.
- **Presenting a copy as the official project.** Governed by this file. Partly enforceable,
  and only if we are honest about the boundary — which is what the rest of this page is.

If you are here because you found something claiming to be us, jump to
[Reporting impersonation](#reporting-impersonation).

### The marks

| Mark | Status |
|:--|:--|
| **SoccerDream** / **SoccerDream AI** | Used as an unregistered mark |
| **SoccerDream Expert Team** (the expert-team naming convention) | Used as an unregistered mark |
| Logos and visual assets in `docs/assets/` | All rights reserved, not licensed |

**Registration status, stated plainly:** these marks are **not registered** in any
jurisdiction at the time of writing. That matters, because registration is what converts a
name into a right you can sue on. Without it, our practical tools are copyright complaints,
platform impersonation policies, and — where the conduct is deceptive — unfair-competition
claims. Not a trademark lawsuit.

We would rather write that sentence than imply a protection we do not have.

### What a fork must do

If you fork and publish, all three, not one:

1. **Rename.** Remove the marks from the repository name, package names, CLI binary, and any
   user-facing text.
2. **Disclose.** Say plainly in your README: *"Unofficial fork of SoccerDream Expert Team.
   Not affiliated with or endorsed by SoccerDream AI."*
3. **Do not imply endorsement.** No "official", no "powered by", no logo in your header.

Keeping the name while distributing a modified version is the one thing that turns a
permitted fork into something we will act on.

### What we cannot stop, and will not pretend to

| Not enforceable | Why |
|:--|:--|
| Someone reading this repository and re-implementing the ideas | Ideas are not copyrightable. The *expression* is; the concept is not. |
| A compliant fork that renamed and disclosed | That is exactly what the licence permits. |
| Someone copying the four-stage protocol into their own product | See row one. The protocol is *documented* on purpose. |
| Anyone charging money for a course about this design | Not our right to control. |

What we can act on is narrower and worth stating precisely: **verbatim or near-verbatim
republication of these files without attribution, and use of the marks to pass something off
as ours.**

### Official channels

Only two. Anything else is not us.

| Official | Address |
|:--|:--|
| Source | `github.com/SoccerDreamAI` |
| Product | `soccerdreamai.com` |

Signals that something is **not** official:

- It asks you for an API key, token, seed phrase or password in an issue, a DM, or a form
- It sells "official" access to this repository's contents
- It uses our marks but does not link to the two addresses above
- Its repository has no `NOTICE.md`, and its licence is MIT or Apache-2.0 — those grant
  commercial rights we never granted

### Reporting impersonation

Email **dengkaijava@126.com** with the subject prefix `[TRADEMARK]`.

Include: the URL, a screenshot with a visible timestamp, and one sentence on how it presents
itself as ours. We acknowledge within 5 working days.

What we will then do, in order: request a correction, file a DMCA or platform
impersonation report, and — if the conduct is commercial and deceptive — take advice on
further steps. What we will not do is threaten anyone who has complied with the licence.

This page is a policy statement, not legal advice to you. If you need to know your own
position, ask a lawyer in your jurisdiction.

## 简体中文

### 这份文件为什么存在

源码可见的仓库会招来两件性质完全不同的事，必须分开回答：

- **抄文件。** 由 `LICENSE` 与 `NOTICE.md` 管。这一条可执行，因为我们持有著作权。
- **拿一份拷贝冒充官方。** 由本文件管。这一条只能部分执行——前提是我们对边界说实话，
  下面写的就是这个边界。

如果你是因为发现了冒名项目才点进来的，直接跳到 [举报仿冒](#举报仿冒)。

### 标识

| 标识 | 状态 |
|:--|:--|
| **SoccerDream** / **SoccerDream AI** | 作为未注册商标使用 |
| **SoccerDream Expert Team**（专家团命名体系） | 作为未注册商标使用 |
| `docs/assets/` 中的 Logo 与视觉素材 | 保留一切权利，不授予许可 |

**注册状态，直说：** 截至本文件撰写时，上述标识**未在任何司法辖区完成注册**。这一点很关键——
注册才是把"名字"变成"可以据以起诉的权利"的那一步。没有它，我们实际可用的手段是著作权投诉、
平台仿冒规则，以及在存在欺骗性时的不正当竞争主张，**而不是商标侵权诉讼**。

我们宁可把这句话写出来，也不愿暗示一种我们并不拥有的保护。

### Fork 必须做的三件事

如果你 fork 并对外发布，三条都要，缺一条都不行：

1. **改名。** 从仓库名、包名、CLI 二进制名、以及任何面向用户的文案里移除本标识。
2. **声明。** 在你的 README 里明写：*「本项目是 SoccerDream Expert Team 的非官方 fork，
   与 SoccerDream AI 无关联、未获其背书。」*
3. **不得暗示背书。** 不许用"官方""powered by"，不许在我们的标识出现在你的页头。

**保留名称同时分发修改版**，是把一个被允许的 fork 变成我们会采取行动的那一件事。

### 我们拦不住的，不假装能拦

| 拦不住 | 原因 |
|:--|:--|
| 有人读了本仓库，然后自己重新实现一遍思路 | 思想不受著作权保护。受保护的是**表达**，不是概念 |
| 一个按规定改名并声明的 fork | 那正是许可所允许的 |
| 别人把四段式流程抄进自己的产品 | 同第一行。这套流程是**刻意公开**的 |
| 有人拿这套设计去开课收费 | 这不归我们管 |

我们真正能主张的范围更窄，但值得写准：**逐字或近逐字转载这些文件而不署名，以及用本标识
把别人的东西说成是我们的。**

### 官方渠道

只有两个。其他都不是我们。

| 官方 | 地址 |
|:--|:--|
| 源码 | `github.com/SoccerDreamAI` |
| 产品 | `soccerdreamai.com` |

**不是官方**的信号：

- 在 issue、私信或表单里向你索要 API Key、token、助记词或密码
- 出售本仓库内容的"官方"获取权
- 用了我们的标识，却不链向上面两个地址
- 仓库里没有 `NOTICE.md`，且许可证是 MIT 或 Apache-2.0——那授予了我们从未授予的商业权利

之后的处理顺序：要求更正 → 提交 DMCA 或平台仿冒举报 → 若涉及商业欺骗，就后续步骤征询专业意见。
我们**不会**去威胁一个遵守了许可的人。
