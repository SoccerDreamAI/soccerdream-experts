You also grant a patent license covering patent claims you own that are necessarily

### 3. Why the relicense grant is required

This project is dual-model: source-available today, Apache-2.0 after the Change Date, and
commercially licensable in between. Those three depend on the same thing — the ability to
apply a different license to the same code later. Without a relicense grant:

- the project could not be converted to Apache-2.0 on schedule, because your lines would
  stay under BUSL;
- a commercial license could not cover the parts you wrote.

You are free to decline. Contributions without this grant cannot be merged, and we will say
so plainly rather than merge first and discover the problem in two years.

### 4. You confirm you have the right to grant it

You confirm that:

- the Contribution is your original work, or you have sufficient rights to submit it;
- it does not knowingly infringe any third party's intellectual property;
- if your employer has rights in work you create, you have permission to submit it, or your
  employer has waived those rights;
- the Contribution contains no credentials, no third-party code without attribution, and no
  material you are contractually barred from publishing.

### 5. What we will not do

- We will not relicense your Contribution under terms that remove your attribution from the
  repository history.
- We will not use this grant to claim you endorse this project or any commercial product.

### 6. No warranty from you

Contributions are provided "as is". You are not liable for any damage arising from your
Contribution, except for breach of the confirmations in §4.

### 7. How to agree

Add this line to your pull request description:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to its terms.
```

That is the whole mechanism. There is no separate signature flow, no bot to install, and no
form to fill in. A reviewer will not merge without it.

本项目是三段式模型：今天是源码可见，Change Date 之后转 Apache-2.0，之间可商业授权。

- 项目无法按期转为 Apache-2.0，因为你写的那些行会一直停在 BUSL 下；

- 贡献中不含凭据、不含未署名的第三方代码、不含你因合同义务不能公开的材料。

- 我们不会利用本授权声称你为本项目或任何商业产品背书。

贡献按「现状」提供。除违反 §4 的确认事项外，你不对因你的贡献引起的任何损害承担责任。

