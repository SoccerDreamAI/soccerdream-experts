# Code of Conduct · 行为准则

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

---

## English

### The short version

Be direct about the work and generous about the person. Attack arguments, not authors.

### Expected

- Critique the reasoning, and say specifically which sentence you disagree with.
- Assume the other person has read the code, even when it is obvious they have not.
- Say "I was wrong" plainly when you were. It costs nothing and it is the fastest way to
  end a thread.
- Disagreement on design is the point of this project. A well-argued "this whole approach
  is wrong" is welcome.

### Not tolerated

- Personal attacks, dismissiveness, or sarcasm aimed at a contributor rather than a claim.
- "Just read the docs" as a complete response. Link the section.
- Bad-faith demands: insisting on a change while refusing to engage with the reason it was
  declined.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information.

### Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where you
are representing the project.

### Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via the email in the repository profile, or via a private
advisory if you prefer. Reports are read by a human, usually within 48 hours.

Responses escalate: a clarifying note, a formal warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban.
We will tell you which one and why. We will not debate it in public.

There is no appeals committee. There is a maintainer who will read what you wrote and make
a call. That is not perfect, but it is honest about what it is.

## 简体中文

### 一句话版本

对事直接，对人宽容。攻击论点，不攻击作者。

### 期望的行为

- 批评推理过程，并且**具体指出你不同意哪一句**。
- 默认对方读过代码——哪怕很明显他没读。
- 错了就直接说「我错了」。零成本，而且这是终止争论最快的方式。
- 围绕设计的分歧正是这个项目存在的意义。一句论证充分的「这整套思路都是错的」，非常欢迎。

### 不可接受的行为

- 针对贡献者本人的攻击、轻慢，或对着人而不是对着主张的讽刺。
- 只回一句「自己去看文档」。请把章节链接贴出来。
- 不讲道理的要求：一边坚持要改，一边拒绝回应「为什么被拒」。
- 任何形式的骚扰，公开或私下。
- 公开他人隐私信息。

### 适用范围

适用于 Issue、Pull Request、Discussions、提交信息，以及任何你代表本项目发言的场合。

### 执行

通过仓库资料里的邮箱联系维护者，或走私有安全通告。**由人来看**，通常 48 小时内。

处理逐级升级：提示说明 → 正式警告 → 临时封禁 → 永久封禁。我们会告诉你用了哪一级、为什么。
**不会在公开场合辩论这个决定。**

这里没有申诉委员会。有的是一个会读完你写的东西然后做判断的维护者。这不完美，但至少它诚实地承认了自己是什么。
